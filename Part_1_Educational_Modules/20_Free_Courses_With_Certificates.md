# Free AI Courses With Completion Certificates

This is a curated list of courses that pass both repository requirements:

1. The learning access is free, with no paid subscription required to complete the course.
2. The provider states that a completion certificate or digital credential is issued after completion.

## Verification Policy

- **Checked:** September 11, 2026.
- **Evidence standard:** each link points to the provider's course or official catalog page, where the free access and completion credential are described.
- **Credential note:** a completion certificate or digital credential proves course completion; it is not the same as an accredited university degree or a professional license.
- **Access note:** platforms can change enrollment rules. Always read the current course page before starting. If a provider changes the certificate to a paid add-on, remove or quarantine the entry rather than keeping an outdated recommendation.

## Recommended Order

For someone building an LLM from scratch, use the resources in this order:

1. AI and machine learning foundations.
2. Neural networks and deep learning.
3. TensorFlow or Keras implementation.
4. Generative AI, RAG, and modern LLM application concepts.
5. The repository's from-scratch implementation and evaluation modules.

## Sources Excluded

The following types of resources are intentionally not listed as qualifying certificate courses:

- Courses that are free to audit but charge for the certificate.
- YouTube videos and playlists without a provider-issued completion credential.
- Platforms that issue only a progress badge when the user specifically asked for a completion certificate.
- Search-result pages, unofficial mirrors, and course aggregators that do not control the credential.

You can still find excellent no-certificate learning material in the repository's [YouTube Learning Roadmap](../../README.md#youtube-learning-roadmap). This file is the stricter certificate-qualified list.

## Maintenance Checklist

When adding a new course, verify all of the following on the provider page:

- The enrollment and learning access are free from start to finish.
- The certificate or digital credential is included, not a paid upgrade.
- The provider names the credential and explains how it is issued.
- The course is relevant to AI, machine learning, deep learning, LLMs, or AI engineering.
- The direct URL is stable and was checked on the date recorded above.
