TOP AI RESEARCH PAPERS (PART 1 / 50)
1. Attention Is All You Need (2017)

https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762

This is the most important AI paper ever written. It introduced the Transformer architecture which removed RNNs completely and replaced them with self-attention.

Key ideas:

Self-attention mechanism
Multi-head attention
Positional encoding

Impact:

Foundation of GPT, BERT, LLaMA, Claude, Gemini
2. Deep Residual Learning (ResNet) (2015)

https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.03385

Introduced residual connections:

y=F(x)+x

Why important:

Solved vanishing gradient problem
Enabled very deep networks (100+ layers)
3. ImageNet Classification with Deep CNN (AlexNet) (2012)

https://papers.nips.cc/paper/2012/hash/c399862d3b9d6b76c8436e924a68c45b-Abstract.html

First major deep learning breakthrough in vision.

Key ideas:

ReLU activation
GPU training
Data augmentation
4. BERT (2018)

https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.04805

Introduced bidirectional transformer pretraining.

Key innovation:

Masked Language Modeling (MLM)

Impact:

Revolutionized NLP understanding tasks
5. GPT (Generative Pretrained Transformer) (2018)

https://openai.com/research/language-unsupervised

Introduced:

Unsupervised pretraining + fine-tuning

Became foundation of all GPT models.

6. GPT-2 (2019)

https://openai.com/research/better-language-models

Showed:

Scaling improves performance dramatically
Zero-shot learning emerges
7. GPT-3 (2020)

https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.14165

Key concept:

Few-shot learning

Insight:

Bigger models = better general intelligence
8. Adam Optimizer (2014)

https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.6980

Most used optimizer in deep learning.

Update rule:

Adaptive learning rates per parameter
9. Batch Normalization (2015)

https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.03167

Solved:

Training instability
Internal covariate shift

Result:

Faster training + higher accuracy
10. Dropout (2014)

https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v15/srivastava14a.html

Prevents overfitting by randomly dropping neurons during training.
