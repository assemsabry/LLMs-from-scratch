# 7. Optimizers

Optimizers are the algorithms that adjust the weights of the neural network in order to minimize the Loss Function.

---

The most common optimizers are:

*   **SGD (Stochastic Gradient Descent):** The classic, foundational optimization algorithm.
*   **Adam (Adaptive Moment Estimation):** A highly popular optimizer that adapts the learning rate for each weight individually.
*   **AdamW:** The absolute best optimizer for deep learning and Large Language Models, as it improves the mathematical regularization of standard Adam.
