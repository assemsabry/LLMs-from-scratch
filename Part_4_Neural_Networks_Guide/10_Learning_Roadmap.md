# 10. Neural Networks Learning Roadmap

To master neural networks, follow this structured, sequential path.

---

*   **Phase 1:** Perceptron $\rightarrow$ FNN $\rightarrow$ Backpropagation
*   **Phase 2:** CNN + Computer Vision
*   **Phase 3:** RNN + LSTM
*   **Phase 4:** Transformers
*   **Phase 5:** LLMs + Training + Fine-tuning
